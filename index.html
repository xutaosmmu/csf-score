Cerebrospinal System Failure Score (CSF Score)

A prognostic scoring system for leptomeningeal metastasis (LM) from non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The acronym CSF carries a deliberate double meaning: Cerebrospinal System Failure — the pathophysiological framework — and cerebrospinal fluid, the biological medium at the center of the disease. Five dimensions capture the cascade from tumor-driven burden to system-level failure.

Karnofsky Performance Status (KPS)
Assessed at time of LM diagnosis. KPS < 60 activates the dual gatekeeper — patient is classified directly as High Risk regardless of total score.
★ KPS < 60 — Dual Gatekeeper Activated
Patient classified directly as High Risk regardless of total score. Remaining dimensions are locked; further scoring is not required for risk stratification.
LM-specific core neurological deficit (LM-CND)
At least one major cranial nerve or spinal cord deficit directly attributable to LM — not pre-existing or from parenchymal metastases.
Extracranial disease progression at LM diagnosis
Systemic disease status at or immediately before the qualifying lumbar puncture, reflecting remaining systemic treatment options.
D2
Molecular Engine
0 – 2 pts
CNS-penetrant TKI availability with LM-specific evidence
Score reflects therapeutic access, not mutation status alone. Zhang et al. (Front Oncol 2022) showed TKI generation superseded raw genomic status — treatment access is the dominant prognostic driver in the modern era.
D3
Anatomical Burden
0 – 2 pts
MRI leptomeningeal enhancement pattern
Classified per EANO-ESMO typology (Le Rhun et al., Ann Oncol 2017). Assessed on contrast-enhanced T1 or FLAIR on baseline MRI at LM diagnosis.
Parenchymal brain metastases (concurrent)
Based on baseline MRI at LM diagnosis. Miliary pattern (innumerable tiny lesions) counts as ≥ 4 regardless of exact lesion count.
D4
Fluid Dynamics
0 – 2 pts
Evans Index (EI) on baseline MRI
EI = maximum width of frontal horns ÷ maximum internal biparietal skull diameter (same axial slice). Interobserver κ = 0.63 (Le Rhun et al., Neuro-Oncology 2022). Threshold ≥ 0.27 validated as prognostically significant in LM (Le Rhun et al., Neurology 2024).
CSF opening pressure / symptomatic hydrocephalus
Measured at diagnostic lumbar puncture (lateral decubitus, fully relaxed). Score 1 pt if OP > 250 mmH₂O or if clinical + radiographic symptomatic hydrocephalus prompted neurosurgical consultation — regardless of recorded OP.
D5
Biological Toxicity
0 – 1 pt
Tumor Marker Index (TMI) > 3
TMI = CSF tumor marker ÷ paired serum marker (same episode). Marker hierarchy: CEA (adenocarcinoma) → CYFRA 21-1 (squamous) → NSE (neuroendocrine). Dual threshold distinguishes active intrathecal secretion from passive blood-CSF barrier leakage (which typically yields ratios < 2–3).
