Cerebral Spinal System Failure Score

A prognostic scoring system for leptomeningeal metastasis (LM) from non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The acronym CSF is intentional: it designates both Cerebral Spinal (system) Failure — the conceptual framework — and cerebrospinal fluid, the biological medium at the center of the disease process. Score five pathophysiologically distinct dimensions to stratify patients into low, intermediate, or high risk groups.
